The capability-only analysis excludes calls classified as API failures. Here, all 17 such records were responses with no visible answer text: 14 from GLM and 3 from Claude. They were retained as zeroes in the all-call mean.

Claude and GPT were not clearly separated by the paired analysis: Claude minus GPT had a mean difference of 0.0337, a 95% bootstrap interval from -0.0337 to 0.1046, and a Holm-adjusted permutation p-value of 0.3633. GPT produced slightly more correct calls (119 versus 116), while Claude had the higher mean partial-credit score.

The no-text failures were task-clustered: GLM's were on chain-ladder coding tasks, and Claude's were on lognormal VaR/TVaR tasks. This may reflect the 2,000-token output budget and provider/model reasoning behavior, not authentication failure.

## Interpretation limits

- This is a 48-task initial portfolio study, not a universal model ranking.
- AgentRouter controls routing, so the labels do not independently prove the underlying model identities.
- Provider cost was unavailable and is not estimated.
- Model streams ran concurrently, but each route key had only one request in flight at a time.
- Domain mix, grading rules, prompt format, and the fixed output budget materially affect the results.
